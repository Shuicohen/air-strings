Step

    How to play

    Hands

    • Make pinch both hands in empty air, pull apart, let go
    • Pluck swipe a fingertip through a string; faster is louder. Swipes never move strings
    • Move / retune pinch both ends at once, one hand on each; shorter is higher
    • Delete pinch the middle and fling it away

    Mouse

    • Make click and drag on empty space
    • Pluck sweep across a string, or drag across from one
    • Retune drag one end dot (mouse only, for testing)
    • Delete right-click or double-click a string

    C clear · X/Del delete selected · S snap · H help
