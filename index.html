Air Strings
Click and drag to draw a string
How to play

Hands

  • Make pinch with both hands, pull apart, let go
  • Pluck swipe a fingertip through a string; faster is louder
  • Retune pinch an end and drag; shorter is higher
  • Delete pinch the middle and fling it away

Mouse

  • Make click and drag on empty space
  • Pluck sweep across a string, or drag across from one
  • Retune drag an end dot
  • Delete right-click or double-click a string

C clear · X/Del delete selected · S snap · H help
